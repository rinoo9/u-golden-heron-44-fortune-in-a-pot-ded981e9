# Fortune in a Pot

A one-line houseplant fortune entry for the make.fun bounty "Fortune in a Pot". The program `main.py` writes a single fortune to `/output/entry.txt` — the plant on the windowsill will outgrow its pot by spring, and its keeper, who talks to it every morning, will take full credit for it.

> You will outgrow this pot by spring, and your keeper — who talks to you every morning — will take full credit for it.

Run it with `python3 main.py`. It asserts the entry is 1–120 characters, a single line with no leading or trailing whitespace, then writes it with no trailing newline.
